About the collection

Built for biological questions,
not just computer vision.

EmbryoPhenomics develops open tools for measuring organisms through images and video. The collection spans experimental hardware, environmental control, scalable processing, phenotype extraction and downstream analysis.

Each repository is maintained independently. Check its documentation, status and licence before use.
